01 / The global picture

When forests fall,
everyone feels it.

Ecosystems unravel

Clearing forests destroys habitats and disrupts the living systems that depend on them.

Biodiversity is lost

Plants and animals lose the places they need to survive, feed, and reproduce.

The climate shifts

Forests store carbon and help regulate the climate. Their destruction adds pressure to a warming planet.

Communities are affected

People who depend on forests face threats to their homes, livelihoods, and ways of life.

Deforestation / Global impactOne crisis, shared consequences

03 / Why it is global

What happens in a forest
doesn't stay there.

Climate regulation

Forests absorb CO₂ and help stabilize the climate systems that people everywhere rely on.

Connected demand

Global demand for beef, soy, and palm oil can drive land clearing far from where products are consumed.

Our supply chains cross borders. So does our responsibility to protect forests.

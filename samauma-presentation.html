04 / Social Responsibility

Five rules protect
local residents.

  1. ≥60% local employment — Tourism jobs go to community members first
  2. No displacement — Families cannot be evicted from residential housing
  3. Sacred areas protected — Wildlife & cultural zones remain restricted to tourists
  4. 40,000 annual visitor cap — Hard limit prevents overtourism
  5. ≥70% Brazilian sourcing — Food, furniture, supplies circulate locally

Social protection is baked into the business model. It's the strategy, not an afterthought.

Protection by designCommunity first

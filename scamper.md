## Design Thinking

Design thinking is a human-centered approach to solving problems through 
empathy, 
defining needs, 
generating ideas, 
prototyping, and 
testing solutions.

## SCAMPER

SCAMPER is a creativity technique for improving ideas by 
**S**ubstituting, 
**C**ombining, 
**A**dapting, 
**M**odifying, 
**P**utting to another use, 
**E**liminating, and 
**R**earranging or 
**R**everse-engineering.

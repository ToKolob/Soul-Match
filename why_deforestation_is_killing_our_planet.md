The Environmental Impact of Human Greed

🌳 Slide 1 – Global Deforestation

Deforestation destroys ecosystems, threatens biodiversity, and accelerates climate change, affecting communities and future generations worldwide.

🇧🇷🇮🇩 Slide 2 – Brazil vs. Indonesia

🇧🇷 Amazon Rainforest: Cattle farming, agriculture, illegal logging, and mining.

🇮🇩 Borneo & Sumatra: Palm oil plantations, logging, and paper production.

Same consequences: Habitat destruction, biodiversity loss, and increased carbon emissions.

🌎 Slide 3 – Why Is It a Global Issue?

Forests regulate the climate and absorb CO₂.

Global demand for beef, soy, and palm oil drives deforestation, making it an international responsibility.

💰 Slide 4 – The Common Root: Human Greed

Despite different industries, both regions face the same problem: profit over environmental protection.

Corporate interests and excessive consumption often prioritise economic growth over nature and local communities.

More production. More profit. Less forest.

💡 Slide 5 – Our Solution

Global Forest Protection Initiative
	•	🛰️ Monitor deforestation using satellites.
	•	🌱 Support reforestation and local communities.
	•	♻️ Promote sustainable production and consumption.

🤝 Slide 6 – Who Must Take Action?
	•	Governments: Enforce environmental laws.
	•	Businesses: Adopt sustainable practices.
	•	Organisations: Provide funding and support.
	•	Individuals: Make responsible choices.

🌱 Slide 7 – Global Impact

Protecting forests means:
	•	Reducing carbon emissions.
	•	Preserving biodiversity.
	•	Protecting Indigenous communities.
	•	Fighting climate change.

Protecting forests means protecting our future.

🌍 Slide 8 – A Question of Priorities

Deforestation reflects a global economic system that often values short-term profits over long-term survival.

Real progress requires balancing economic development with environmental responsibility.

💚 Final Message

“Nature provides enough for everyone’s needs, but not for everyone’s greed.”

— Commonly attributed to Mahatma Gandhi
